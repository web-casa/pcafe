### `EditionPicker` Edition picker (Options - introducing a few editions or plans one by one)
- Use when: A product's editions, plans or releases, each with its own use; Going through them one by one, saying who each one fits
- Avoid: Prices must be compared: use PricingTiers; More than 6 options: split the slide or use FilterChips
- Limits: options 3–6; name ≤ 12 Latin characters or ≤ 8 chars; tag ≤ 6 chars; desc ≤ 40 chars (≤ 30 chars with 6 options); fit ≤ 24 chars (≤ 14 chars with 6 options)
- Reference: src/decks/v3-p1 slide `editions`; docs/en/components.md#edition-picker; interaction: With build: 'focus', each press → the selection moves to the next card
