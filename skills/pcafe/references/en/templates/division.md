### `Team` Team cards (Roles - who owns what)
- Use when: Introducing the team at a kick-off; Roles in a launch team or a virtual team
- Avoid: Each task needs its owner and its decider spelled out: build a RACI in Matrix; Only 2–3 people: put them in body text
- Limits: members 4, 6 or 8; name ≤ 8 chars; role ≤ 14 chars; owns ≤ 36 chars; initials 1–3 characters; when empty, the first character of the name or the English initials are used; No photo slot: real photos do not go into a deck
- Reference: src/decks/gtm slide `team`; docs/en/components.md#team; interaction: Cards appear one after another; hover lifts a card.

### `Swimlane` Swimlane flowchart (Roles - who does which step in a process)
- Use when: A process across roles, a handoff; 4–8 steps
- Avoid: Only one role: use StepTrack; You need long-term role division: use Lanes
- Limits: lanes 2–4, each ≤ 6 chars; steps 4–8, text ≤ 10 chars
- Reference: src/decks/wallpaper slide `swimlane`; docs/en/components.md#swimlane
