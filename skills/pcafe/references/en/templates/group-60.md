### `FlipCards` Flip cards (Reveal - question first, answer after)
- Use when: Correct a misconception: the common claim on the front, the fact on the back; Pose a question for the audience to guess, then flip to reveal
- Avoid: No question-then-answer suspense: use PanelCards; More than 4 cards: split the slide
- Limits: cards 2–4; tag ≤ 6 chars; front ≤ 3 lines × 9 chars; title ≤ 12 chars; body ≤ 48 chars; backTag ≤ 6 chars; The flip needs build: reveal
- Reference: src/decks/v2-p3 slide `myths`; docs/en/components.md#flip-cards; interaction: build: reveal flips one card per step; without steps or in print every card shows its back

### `Envelope` Envelope (Reveal - a letter or an announcement)
- Use when: Quoting a letter, a notice or an announcement; You want the reveal feel of opening something up
- Avoid: The letter text is over 72 chars: use QuoteHero; The content is invented and unmarked: mark it illustrative in sign or source
- Limits: title ≤ 3 lines × 8 chars; lead ≤ 60 chars; greeting ≤ 12 chars; body ≤ 72 chars; sign ≤ 14 chars; stamp ≤ 4 characters; texture only takes a decor- paper texture
- Reference: src/decks/v2-p3 slide `stamp`; docs/en/components.md#envelope; interaction: build: on reveal, step 1 opens the flap and step 2 pulls out the letter

### `ExplorerFlip` Browser flip (Reveal - how it looks vs what it is)
- Use when: Explain a data structure that looks like A but is B (prefixes, aliases, mappings); There are 3–6 concrete entries
- Avoid: Only showing a file list: use Screenshot or Terminal; More than 6 entries
- Limits: path ≤ 32 Latin characters; rows 3–6: name ≤ 24 Latin characters (a trailing / draws a folder), key ≤ 40 Latin characters, size ≤ 10 characters; views each ≤ 6 chars; note ≤ 60 chars
- Reference: src/decks/v3-p5 slide `flat`; docs/en/components.md#explorer-flip; interaction: build: reveal — step 1 flips to the keys, step 2 shows the note; the complete state shows the keys
