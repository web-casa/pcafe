### `IssueTree` Issue tree (Analysis - breaking the question apart)
- Use when: A review or diagnosis: why a metric moved; The first slide of a consulting-style analysis: split the question into parts that do not overlap
- Avoid: The branches have an order: use FlowDiagram; The answer is known: give it with BigStat or NumberedColumns
- Limits: branches 2–4; leaves 2–3 each; root.title ≤ 12 chars; note ≤ 16 chars; branch.title ≤ 10 chars; note ≤ 16 chars; leaf ≤ 14 chars (≤ 10 chars with 4 branches); Branches must not overlap and together must cover the whole question
- Reference: src/decks/gtm slide `diagnose`; docs/en/components.md#issue-tree; interaction: Branches appear one after another, cause tags pop in after them; hovering a branch turns its card to a light accent.

### `DecisionTree` Decision tree (Analysis - which one to pick when)
- Use when: Choosing an option or a tool from conditions; Every question can be answered yes / no
- Avoid: More than two levels of questions: split it over two slides; You need to break down causes: use IssueTree
- Limits: q ≤ 14 chars, leaf ≤ 8 chars, note ≤ 14 chars; At most two levels of questions, 4 results
- Reference: src/decks/toy3d slide `chart-pick`; docs/en/components.md#decision-tree

### `Fishbone` Fishbone diagram (Analysis - causes of one problem)
- Use when: Troubleshooting a problem, reviewing an incident; The causes fall into 4–6 categories
- Avoid: The causes have cause chains: use IssueTree; Fewer than 4 categories
- Limits: problem ≤ 10 chars; bones 4 or 6: label ≤ 6 chars, causes 1–3, each ≤ 12 chars
- Reference: src/decks/wallpaper slide `fishbone`; docs/en/components.md#fishbone
