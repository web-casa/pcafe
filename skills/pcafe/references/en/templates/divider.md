### `SectionBreak` Section break (Section break - first slide of each chapter)
- Use when: Pairs with Agenda; one at the start of each chapter; Where the talk needs a pause or a change of subject
- Avoid: A short deck without an Agenda; Two section breaks in a row; A chapter with one slide of content: no separate break needed
- Limits: title 1–2 lines, ≤ 6 chars per line (128u size); lead ≤ 44 chars; contents 2–4 items, ≤ 12 chars each, the titles of the chapter's slides; Pick an icon that stands for the whole chapter; it takes the accent colour; No source field
- Reference: src/decks/geo slide `ch2`; docs/en/components.md#section-break; interaction: Concentric circles unfold in order and the chapter number rises digit by digit; the icon drifts with the mouse.

### `ChapterNumber` Chapter number (Section break - a huge chapter number with a short title)
- Use when: The start of a chapter; Give the audience a sense of place in a long deck
- Avoid: The chapter has no number: use SectionBreak; You need to list the chapter's key points: use ChapterProgress
- Limits: num 1–2 characters; title 2–3 lines, ≤ 8 chars per line; body ≤ 60 chars; foot two items, ≤ 16 characters each
