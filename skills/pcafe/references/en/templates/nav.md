### `Agenda` Contents (Navigation - after the cover)
- Use when: A deck of more than 15 slides with several chapters; The audience should see the shape first, or you need to jump back to a chapter mid-talk
- Avoid: A short deck under 10 slides: the contents alone takes a tenth of it; Only 2 chapters: say so in the cover lead
- Limits: items 3–6, in deck order; title ≤ 10 chars; body one line, ≤ 26 chars; page is the number of the chapter's first slide (from 1); update it after reordering; meta ≤ 20 characters, e.g. "27 slides · 4 chapters"; No source field: the contents states no facts
- Reference: src/decks/geo slide `agenda`; docs/en/components.md#agenda; interaction: Hover a row: the background brightens, the icon tile takes the accent background and turns slightly, the arrow moves right; click to jump to that chapter's first slide.
