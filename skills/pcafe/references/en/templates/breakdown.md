### `Waterfall` Revenue bridge (Breakdown - from opening to closing)
- Use when: Breaking down how ARR, profit, budget or headcount moved from opening to closing; Showing what ate the growth
- Avoid: The items do not add or subtract: use MetricBars or DeltaBars; More than 6 changes: merge the small ones
- Limits: steps 3–6, a positive number adds, a negative one subtracts; the closing total is computed; label ≤ 6 chars; end ≤ 6 chars; notes 2–3: key ≤ 8 chars (one number allowed), text ≤ 30 chars; Keep values within 5 digits and put the scale in unit, for example ten thousand yuan or thousand people
