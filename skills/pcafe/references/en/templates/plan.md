### `Gantt` Gantt chart (Plan - work laid out by week)
- Use when: A 30–90 day action plan or a pilot schedule; Several work streams overlap and the order has to be clear
- Avoid: A long period with real dates (support window, contract term): use Timeline; Only order, no duration: use StepTrack
- Limits: axis 6–16 units; groups 2–4 spans; rows 3–7: label ≤ 12 chars; sort by start; With a bar shorter than 2 units the “5–9” label inside it does not fit; footnote ≤ 40 chars
