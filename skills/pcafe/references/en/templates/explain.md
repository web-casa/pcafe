### `Screenshot` Annotated screenshot (Explanation - product interface)
- Use when: Introducing a product interface, a site structure, how to read a report; The audience has to find a place on the real interface
- Avoid: No real screenshot: do not pass a diagram off as one; Two states need comparing: use BeforeAfter; More than 5 points: split into two slides
- Limits: callouts 3–5; x and y are percentages, dots at least 8 apart; title ≤ 10 chars; body ≤ 36 chars; Keep images in the deck folder and import them; prefer WebP, ≤ 200 KB each; Set ratio to the real aspect ratio; url is optional and shows the browser address bar when set
- Reference: src/decks/geo slide `entry`; docs/en/components.md#screenshot; interaction: Dots pop in one by one with a breathing ring; hovering a dot or its note highlights both. In build steps the dot and its note appear together.

### `PhotoSplit` Half image, half text (Explanation - one image with one block of text)
- Use when: Introduce an object or concept where an image helps; A chapter needs a slide that breathes

- Limits: title ≤ 3 lines × 9 chars; lead ≤ 60 chars; points 0–3: title ≤ 10 chars, body ≤ 36 chars; With a lead, use ≤ 3 points; when the bodies are full, prefer ≤ 2
- Reference: src/decks/v2-p1 slide `animal`; docs/en/components.md#photo-split; interaction: build: points appear one by one
