### `ClaimCards` Claim check (Claim check - a verdict per claim)
- Use when: Clearing up a misunderstanding that circulates in the field; Giving each claim in a set a level of evidence
- Avoid: Only one or two: put them in another slide's lead; A verdict has no source: the whole value of this slide is the evidence
- Limits: claims exactly 4 or 6 (2 × 2 or 3 × 2); claim ≤ 22 chars, written the way people say it; the template adds quotation marks; verdict one of five: false (wrong), unsupported (no evidence), disputed (contested), partial (partly backed), supported (backed); note ≤ 36 chars, the single most important piece of evidence; tag ≤ 16 characters, an English topic word
- Reference: src/decks/geo slide `claims`; docs/en/components.md#claim-cards; interaction: Stamps land one by one; a false claim is then struck through with an accent line. Hovering a card lifts it.
