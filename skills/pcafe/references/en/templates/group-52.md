- Use when: Beginner questions, user questions; Making Q&A read like a conversation

### `FaqAccordion` Accordion Q&A (Q&A - open one at a time)
- Use when: 3–6 common questions you want to go through one by one; The answers are short, one or two sentences
- Avoid: You want all questions and answers at once: use Faq; The questions are steps: use ProcessAccordion or StepTrack
- Limits: items 3–6; q ≤ 24 chars (≤ 18 chars on one line); a ≤ 70 chars; title 1–2 lines, ≤ 8 chars per line; lead ≤ 50 chars; Print and overview show only the one item set by open
- Reference: src/decks/v3-p1 slide `faq`; docs/en/components.md#faq-accordion; interaction: With build: 'focus': each press opens the next item and closes the previous one
