### `PricingTiers` Tiered pricing (Pricing - good / better / best)
- Use when: Product tiers and a pricing plan; Steering most customers to one tier
- Avoid: Comparing competitor prices: use Matrix; Only one tier: use BigStat
- Limits: tiers 3 or 4, from cheapest to most expensive; name ≤ 6 characters; price ≤ 6 characters; unit ≤ 10 characters; for ≤ 16 chars; features 3–5, each ≤ 16 chars; the next tier starts with “includes everything in the tier above”; focus exactly 1 tier; badge ≤ 6 chars; footnote ≤ 50 chars
- Reference: src/decks/gtm slide `tiers`; docs/en/components.md#pricing-tiers; interaction: Cards rise one after another; hover lifts a card.

### `GlowPricing` Glow pricing (Pricing - three plans)
- Use when: Pricing for a subscription product; You want to show live that yearly saves money
- Avoid: More than 3 plans or a line-by-line comparison: use PricingTiers or CompareTable; Only one price: use BigStat
- Limits: title 1 line ≤ 12 chars; periods 2 × ≤ 4 chars, save ≤ 6 chars; plans exactly 3: name ≤ 4 chars, each price ≤ 6 characters, body ≤ 24 chars, items 3–5 × ≤ 10 chars, cta ≤ 6 chars; quota optional: label ≤ 6 chars, value ≤ 10 characters, pct 0–100
- Reference: src/decks/sig-nocturne slide `pricing`; docs/en/components.md#glow-pricing; interaction: Click: switches the billing period and every price

### `BuyoutPricing` Buyout pricing (Pricing - two plans and a close)
- Use when: One-time purchase with two plans; Price and final call to action on the same slide
- Avoid: Subscription with monthly / yearly: use GlowPricing or PricingTiers; More than 2 plans: use PricingTiers
- Limits: title 1 line ≤ 10 chars; plans exactly 2: name ≤ 4 chars, price ≤ 6 characters, unit ≤ 4 chars, body ≤ 20 chars, items 3–4 × ≤ 10 chars, facts 2 (label ≤ 4 chars, value ≤ 6 chars), cta ≤ 6 chars, badge ≤ 3 chars; close.title ≤ 2 lines × 6 chars, body ≤ 36 chars, points 2–3 × ≤ 12 chars, count.value ≤ 7 characters, count.label ≤ 6 chars, cta ≤ 6 chars
- Reference: src/decks/sig-sunrise slide `pricing`; docs/en/components.md#buyout-pricing; interaction: Click: switches the picked plan, its button turns the theme colour
