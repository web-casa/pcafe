### `PillSentence` Sentence with icon pills (Point of view - one big line that states the problem)

### `RotatingWord` Rotating word (Point of view - swap 2–4 words in one sentence)
- Use when: Parallel answers like “it comes down to X / Y / Z”; One sentence frame takes 2–4 objects in turn
- Avoid: The words share no sentence frame: use IconGrid or cards; Values must be compared: use a chart
- Limits: before ≤ 8 chars, after ≤ 6 chars; words 2–4: word ≤ 4 chars, value ≤ 8 characters, note ≤ 30 chars
- Reference: src/decks/v2-p2 slide `stack`; docs/en/components.md#rotating-word; interaction: in build mode each → press moves to the next word and lights its card; the final state shows the last word
