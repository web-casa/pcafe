### `MediaExpand` Small window expand (Motion - a screenshot or photo grows from a small window to the full slide)
- Use when: Product demos and cases: let people recognise the screen first, then look at the details; One image is the point of the slide
- Avoid: The screenshot needs annotations: use Screenshot; The subject sits near the image edge and would be cropped when expanded
- Limits: title ≤ 2 lines × 10 chars; lead ≤ 40 chars; caption ≤ 24 chars; src is a real screenshot or a photo with its source stated; Use build: 'reveal' (1 step only)
- Reference: src/decks/motion slide `expand`; docs/en/components.md#media-expand; interaction: Press → expand the image

### `ScatterOrder` Scatter to order (Motion - scattered cards fall into place one by one along a path)
- Use when: Turn scattered items into ordered steps; A process, a method or a preparation checklist
- Avoid: The steps loop: use Cycle; Each step needs a detailed explanation: use a Process template
- Limits: steps 4–6; title ≤ 8 chars; body ≤ 28 chars; slide title ≤ 22 chars; lead ≤ 40 chars
- Reference: src/decks/motion slide `scatter`; docs/en/components.md#scatter-order; interaction: Press → cards fall into place one by one

### `TiltShot` Tilt to straight (Motion - a screenshot in perspective straightens, then the points are covered one by one)
- Use when: Introducing the interface of software, a tool or a console; Give the overall impression first, then cover 2–3 highlights
- Avoid: You need to mark positions on the screenshot: use Screenshot; There is no real screenshot
- Limits: title ≤ 2 lines × 10 chars; lead ≤ 40 chars; points 2–3: title ≤ 10 chars, body ≤ 20 chars; src must be a real screenshot
- Reference: src/decks/motion slide `tilt`; docs/en/components.md#tilt-shot; interaction: Press → straighten the screenshot, then reveal the points one by one

### `ConvergeShots` Images converge (Motion - several images stand side by side, then converge into one focus image)
- Use when: Several screens or views all end in one result; Show the range first, then focus on the one to talk about
- Avoid: Every image needs detail: use Screenshot or split the slide; The images differ a lot in aspect ratio
- Limits: shots 3–4, ideally all 16:9; label ≤ 10 chars; caption ≤ 36 chars; slide title ≤ 22 chars; lead ≤ 40 chars; Screenshots are evidence; photos only set the mood
- Reference: src/decks/motion slide `converge`; docs/en/components.md#converge-shots; interaction: Press → converge on the focus image; press again to show the caption

### `StepVideo` Step through video (Motion - each press plays one segment and stops on the frame to talk about)
- Use when: A screen recording or an animated chart you want to talk over while it plays; A change with 2–4 moments worth stopping at
- Avoid: The video is long or large: it is inlined into a single HTML file; You need sound: the video is always muted
- Limits: stops 2–4, at increases in seconds; title ≤ 8 chars; body ≤ 30 chars; slide title ≤ 22 chars; lead ≤ 40 chars; video ideally ≤ 15 s and ≤ 1 MB (trim and compress with ffmpeg first)
- Reference: src/decks/motion slide `video`; docs/en/components.md#step-video; interaction: Press → play to the next stop
