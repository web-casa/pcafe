### `TextArc` Circular text (Atmosphere - one line bent into a ring)
- Use when: A phrase or slogan worth repeating; An editorial section break or impression slide
- Avoid: The ring text must be read closely: put it in the body; No word or number fits the center
- Limits: title ≤ 2 lines × 8 chars; lead ≤ 60 chars; points 0–3, each ≤ 24 chars; ring 16–44 units (a Latin character counts 1, a Chinese character counts 2), padded to a full circle with repeated “ · ”; Latin uppercase works best, Chinese characters turn sideways along the arc; center ≤ 5 characters, centerLabel ≤ 8 chars
- Reference: src/decks/v2-p2 slide `desolation`; docs/en/components.md#text-arc; interaction: the ring is one build step; it rotates into place on appear, then holds still
