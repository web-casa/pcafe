### `Cover` Cover (Opening - slide 1)
- Use when: The first slide of a deck; The subject condenses into one number or short code: a version number, year, quarter, rank
- Avoid: The subject has no number that represents it: put an initial or the last two digits of a year in numeral instead of forcing one; A section break: Teaser is more restrained for that
- Limits: numeral 1–4 characters; at 3–4 characters the size shrinks automatically; title 1–2 lines, ≤ 12 characters per line; subtitle ≤ 10 characters and narrower than numeral, otherwise it crowds the big numeral; facts 2–4 items, each value ≤ 10 characters; note ≤ 2 lines; No source field: any fact on the cover must appear on a later slide with its source
- Reference: src/decks/release slide `cover`; docs/en/components.md#cover; interaction: The numeral follows the mouse slightly; its digits rise out of a mask one by one.
