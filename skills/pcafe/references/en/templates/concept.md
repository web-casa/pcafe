### `QuoteHero` Definition and quotation (Concept - define a term / quote a line)
- Use when: One definition of a term for the whole deck; A weighty original line carries the argument
- Avoid: The sentence runs past 64 chars: take it apart in NumberedColumns; A quotation without a reliable source
- Limits: quote ≤ 64 chars; pass an array for line breaks, at most 3 lines × 22 chars; marks 1–3, each an exact substring of quote; cite ≤ 30 characters, the original source; notes exactly 3: label ≤ 8 chars, text ≤ 36 chars; Quote the original word for word; do not paraphrase and add quotation marks
- Reference: src/decks/geo slide `definition`; docs/en/components.md#quote-hero; interaction: Keyword highlights sweep in from the left one by one; hovering a note turns its icon secondary colour to accent.

### `Venn` Venn diagram (Concept - the overlap of a few things)
- Use when: Several dimensions together decide one result; A qualitative point with no numbers
- Avoid: You need to compare part sizes: use Donut; More than 3 sets
- Limits: sets 2–3: label ≤ 8 chars, note ≤ 10 chars; overlap ≤ 8 chars
- Reference: src/decks/toy3d slide `parts-of-deck`; docs/en/components.md#venn
