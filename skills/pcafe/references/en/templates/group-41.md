### `Statement` One line (Rhythm - a pause between two dense passages)
- Use when: A data slide has just finished and the conclusion needs to stand on its own; A pause is needed mid-chapter so one line sticks
- Avoid: One sentence does not fit: use QuoteHero or BigStat; Two slides in a row: too many pauses stop being a pause
- Limits: text ≤ 28 chars; an array breaks lines by hand, at most 2 lines × 14 chars; marks 0–2, each a substring that appears verbatim in text; sub ≤ 40 chars; Numbers in the sentence need their source in source too; At most 3 slides per deck
- Reference: src/decks/gtm slide `price-point`; docs/en/components.md#statement

### `NumberOpener` Big number opener (Rhythm - Open a chapter with one number)
- Use when: Open with the single most important number; Set the tone of a chapter with one number
- Avoid: Need several numbers: use BigStat or KpiBoard; The number has no source
- Limits: value ≤ 5 characters; unit ≤ 4 characters; text ≤ 2 lines × 14 chars; sub ≤ 40 chars; source required
- Reference: src/decks/launch slide `count`; docs/en/components.md#number-opener

### `ChapterProgress` Chapter progress (Rhythm - First slide of a chapter, shows where the talk is)
- Use when: A long deck in chapters, on the first slide of each chapter; The audience needs to know how much is left
- Avoid: Only one chapter; Want icons and big cards on the section break: use SectionBreak
- Limits: chapters 3–6, each ≤ 8 chars; current starts at 0; title ≤ 2 lines × 10 chars; lead ≤ 50 chars
- Reference: src/decks/launch slide `ch2`; docs/en/components.md#chapter-progress

### `Question` Question slide (Rhythm - Lead into the next part with one question)
- Use when: The next chapter answers one question; Say the audience's doubt out loud first
- Avoid: The question already contains its answer: use Statement; Used twice in a row
- Limits: question ≤ 2 lines × 12 chars, ends with a question mark; sub ≤ 36 chars
- Reference: src/decks/launch slide `why`; docs/en/components.md#question
