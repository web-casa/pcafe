### `FilterChips` Filter chips (Classification - filter one set by tag)
- Use when: A set of items has overlapping categories and you want to go through them one group at a time; There are many items but each needs only one sentence
- Avoid: Categories do not overlap and there are only two or three: use Matrix or group them with IconGrid; Items need a detailed explanation: split the slide
- Limits: chips 2–6, each ≤ 6 chars or ≤ 10 Latin characters; cards 4–12 (4 per row); title ≤ 16 Latin characters or ≤ 8 chars; desc ≤ 22 chars (12 cards: ≤ 16 chars); in holds the chip indexes, at least 1
- Reference: src/decks/v3-p1 slide `variants`; docs/en/components.md#filter-chips; interaction: With build: 'focus': each press switches to the next filter and cards outside that group turn grey
