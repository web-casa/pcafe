### `NumberedColumns` Numbered points (Key points - parallel features)
- Use when: 3–4 equally important features, principles or findings; Each point fits under one keyword tag
- Avoid: The points have an order: use StepTrack; The points differ in weight: use SpotlightList or Bento; More than 4: split the slide
- Limits: items exactly 3 or 4; title ≤ 10 chars (one line); body 24–40 chars: shorter leaves the column empty, longer pushes into the big number at the foot; Put long English identifiers (component names, package names) in tag, not in body; they cannot break mid-word; tag ≤ 20 characters; Keep body lengths close across columns
- Reference: src/decks/release slide `hardening`; docs/en/components.md#numbered-columns; interaction: Hover a whole column: the number turns accent and lifts, and the tag takes the accent background.

### `ToggleCard` Toggle settings window (Key points - which are on, which are off)
- Use when: Default settings, feature switches or options; A deck in the desktop style
- Avoid: The point is whether a state is good or bad: use StatusGrid
- Limits: rows 3–5: label ≤ 10 chars, note ≤ 24 chars; title ≤ 2 lines × 8 chars
- Reference: src/decks/wallpaper slide `toggles`; docs/en/components.md#toggle-card; interaction: Clicking a toggle switches it; illustrative only
