> English translation of `SKILL.md`.

Decks in this project are assembled only from the fixed templates in `src/kit`. Quality depends on two things: **does the shape of each slide's content match its template**, and **does every field stay within the template's content limits**. This skill explains how to achieve both. The authoritative rules are in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/en/guide.md`; they win in case of conflict.

Reference files (generated by `npx pcafe docs`, don't edit by hand; descriptions currently in Chinese):

- `references/en/templates.md`: the template index, one line per group (group, content shapes, template names). Read it first to pick a group.
- `references/en/templates/<group>.md`: each group's "good for / avoid / limits / reference slide". Read only the groups you need, never all of them at once.
- `references/en/styles.md`: all styles (`DeckDef.style`) and colour themes (`DeckDef.theme`).

## If @pcafe/pro is installed

When `@pcafe/pro` is in the project dependencies, its templates and styles come with the package. Export the deck from the package in `src/decks/active.ts`, for example `export { deck } from '@pcafe/pro/decks/<pack>'`; templates import engine APIs only from `pcafe`. With Pro installed, `#/kit` and `npx pcafe docs` list its templates automatically; without it, no Pro content appears.

## Process

Go in order; finish each step before the next.

### 1. Collect facts

List the numbers, dates, names and quotes you will use, with their sources. Facts without a source are left out; estimated or illustrative figures say "illustrative" or "estimate" in `source`. Never invent testimonials, logos or awards.

### 2. Write the storyline

First write the one sentence the audience should remember; it is the `statement` of the final `Closing` slide (≤ 20 units). Then order the slides: short 6–8, full 10–14, chaptered long 20–30 (each chapter opens with `SectionBreak` or `ChapterProgress`). For each slide, write one sentence saying what it proves; that is usually the title.

### 3. Assign a template to each slide: shape first

Don't pick a nice template and stuff it with text. Decide the content's **shape**, find the matching group in the `references/en/templates.md` index, open that group's file (for example `references/en/templates/data.md`) and confirm with its "good for / avoid" lines.

| Content shape | Look at |
| --- | --- |
| One number carries the slide | `BigStat`, `NumberOpener`, `CountCompare` (two values), `Gauge` (vs target) |
| 3–4 parallel numbers | `KpiBoard`, `StatRow`, `SparkCards` (each with history) |
| Share of a whole | `Donut`, `Pictogram` (n out of N), `Waterfall` (breakdown) |
| Ranking / change over time | `BarChart`, `MetricBars`, `LineChart`, `SlopeRank` (rank at two points) |
| Two things compared | `Contrast`, `BeforeAfter`, `DuoCards`, `CompareTable` (row by row), `Balance` (trade-off), `DoubleFunnel` (two groups, same stages) |
| Ordered steps | `StepTrack`, `ServiceIndex`, `WindowStack`, `Swimlane` (across roles) |
| Structure and relations | `Pyramid` (layered dependency), `Concentric` (core to edge), `ArchLayers` (tech layers), `Venn` (overlap), `Orbit` (core with satellites) |
| Causes / decisions | `IssueTree`, `Fishbone` (grouped causes), `DecisionTree` (yes/no), `Matrix`, `Quadrant` |
| Time and plans | `Timeline`, `Milestones`, `Roadmap`, `Gantt` |
| 3–9 parallel points | `IconGrid`, `TileWall`, `NumberedColumns`, `Bento` |
| One sentence / quote | `Statement`, `QuoteHero`, `QuoteArc` (three sourced quotes) |
| Pacing | `SectionBreak`, `Teaser`, `Question`, `Recap` |

Rules:

- A template appears at most twice per deck (`SectionBreak` once per chapter doesn't count); `Bento` at most once.
- No template on two adjacent slides.
- Style-specific templates (e.g. `FloatCover`, `Desktop`, `KeyReveal`) are used in their own style; their "good for" line says which.
- If nothing fits, **change the content to fit a template**: split, merge, change the angle. Don't write a new layout.

### 4. Fit the text to the limits

For every field, check the template's "limit" in its group file. Limits are hard; exceeding them breaks the layout.

- Counting: one CJK character = 1 unit; two Latin letters, digits or spaces = 1 unit. Fields measured in "characters" count each character as 1.
- Titles state a claim, not a topic: "Runs offline from a single file", not "Build output".
- Titles longer than one line are passed as arrays for manual breaks, e.g. `title={['Ship one page,', 'on a solid base']}`; never leave one or two words alone on line two.
- `kicker`: 2–8 units, names the topic. `lead`: one or two sentences adding evidence or context, not repeating the title.
- Numbers carry units and scope; long identifiers go in `tag`, `source` or code fields, not body text.
- If it doesn't fit, split into two slides. **Don't shrink fonts, don't write custom CSS.**
- Column item counts differ by at most 1; don't leave the lower half of a slide empty (add an optional block or use a fuller template).

### 5. Choose style and theme

Read `references/en/styles.md`, pick a style for the occasion (`DeckDef.style`), then a colour theme (`DeckDef.theme`, default `crimson`). If unsure, open `#/kit/styles` to compare.

- Formal reports: `standard`, `editorial`, `calm`, `emerald`.
- Launches and brand: `keynote`, `toy3d`, `poster`, `gradient`, `warm-dark`, `pink-script`.
- Products and tools: `studio-dark`, `bento`, `soft-saas`, `float-ui`, `wallpaper`, `desktop`, `neo-grid`.
- Casual talks: `sticker`, `playful`, `pop`, `ribbon`, `mono-studio`, `block-frame`, `stencil`.
- Journals, annual reports, research briefs: `cobalt-grid`, `emerald`, `forest`, `biennale`.
- Opinion, manifestos, rallying a team: `platform`, `broadside`.
- `cobalt-grid` sets everything in the one theme colour and looks closest to its source with `cobalt`; pair `forest` and `pink-script` with `crimson`, `broadside` with `amber`.
- These styles have their own templates; prefer them when you use that style: `biennale` has `ExhibitCover` (cover) and `YearBars` (figures with yearly bars); `stencil` has `NumberTiles` (numbered colour tiles) and `ScoreTable` (scorecard); `neo-grid` has `MetricMosaic` (tiled figures) and `StepGrid` (next-steps grid); `block-frame` has `FramedStats`; `cobalt-grid` has `IndexList`; `forest` has `AgendaMosaic`; `emerald` has `MethodSteps`; `platform` has `PriorityColumns`; `pink-script` has `ChapterNumber`; `broadside` has `StackBars`. They also work in other styles.

A style changes only fonts, radius, background and decoration; content limits stay the same. In dark-by-default styles, write `tone: 'light'` for a light slide. Per-slide dark / accent tone is only for the templates listed in `docs/en/presenting.md` under "Tone".

### 6. Write the deck file

```tsx
// src/decks/<name>/index.tsx   (create it with: npx pcafe new <name>)
import type { DeckDef } from '../../kit'
import { Cover, StatRow, Closing } from '../../kit'

export const deck: DeckDef = {
  title: 'Deck name',
  theme: 'cobalt',
  style: 'bento',
  iconStyle: 'line-duotone',
  slides: [
    { id: 'cover', label: 'Cover', render: () => <Cover … /> },
    { id: 'numbers', label: 'Scale', build: 'reveal', render: () => <StatRow … source="Source" /> },
  ],
}
```

- Preview with `npx pcafe dev <name>`.
- Compose only templates from `src/kit`; don't modify templates, write colours or CSS.
- Icons: only names listed in the "Icons" section at the end of `docs/en/components.md` (in the browser: `#/kit/icons`).
- Images: real screenshots in the deck's `assets/`, imported. Decorative illustrations are named `decor-…` and never pose as evidence. Stock photos are named `photo-…`, live in `src/assets/photos/` (downloaded with `npx pcafe photos`), set atmosphere only and are credited in `source`.
- Slides that reveal item by item get `build: 'reveal'`.
- Each slide may have `notes` (speaker notes, shown in the presenter view via `S`) and `seconds` (planned speaking time; the presenter view's rehearsal timer compares it with the actual time). Deck-wide `texture` and `transition` are optional; see `docs/en/presenting.md`.
- For a PowerPoint file, run `npx pcafe pptx <name>` → `dist/<name>.pptx`: text becomes editable boxes, the rest of each slide is a background picture (charts and SVG text stay in it, fonts are substituted where missing). See "PowerPoint export" in `docs/en/presenting.md`.

### 7. Check and review screenshots

Checks come in two tiers. Run them yourself; don't wait for the user to ask:

| When | Run | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| While making the deck, after changing some slides | `npx pcafe check <name> --changed` | Checks only slides whose content changed. After a change to shared styles, the style, the theme, slide order or slide count it runs the full check and prints why. `--slides 3,5` picks slides by number |
| Before delivery | `npx pcafe check <name>` | The full check, once. The last line must be `PASS` |

1. A partial check (`--changed`, `--slides`) that passes prints `run a full check before delivery`. It is not the final check: run the full check once before you deliver. Add `--explain` to see which slides would run without checking anything.
2. Fix FAILs as described in `docs/en/guide.md` ("Fixing QA errors"); also look at WARNs such as "lower half looks empty". After a fix, rerun only the slides involved, not the full check after every edit.
3. `slide N never settled` means something on the slide keeps changing. For a real live element (a clock, a ticking number) add `data-settle-ignore`; don't delete it to get past the check.
4. After the full check, look at `qa/contact-sheet.png` first (the whole deck as thumbnails) for pacing and density, then open every `qa/slide-NN-1920x1080.png`: too much white space, overcrowded slides, unclear focus, a title's second line with only one or two words.
5. Emphasis matches the text: "longest" highlights the largest item; "at step 3" fills only up to step 3.

`hint  copy.…` lines before `PASS` flag stock phrases; they are not failures. Rewrite them, or keep them when accurate; "studies show" needs a named `source`.

Before the full check, run Simplified Chinese copy (titles, leads, points) through `skills/qu-ai-wei/SKILL.md` as an embedded step: wording only, no new facts, numbers, quotes, commands and `source` untouched, still within each template's limits. For English copy, write plain sentences without stock phrases. When the user only asks to de-AI a piece of Chinese text, use that skill's normal mode.

Add `--json` to any check to get only the issues (stable codes) and a `next` step; `npx pcafe status [format] <name> --json` gives the next step from the last check. See `docs/en/guide.md`, "Reports for scripts and agents".

Skip checks only when the user explicitly asks for a quick draft, and say in your reply that it is unchecked. `npm run qa:kit` and `npm run check:engine` are the engine maintainers' self-tests; don't run them for a deck.

Done means a full-check `PASS` and every screenshot opened and looked at. When you report back, say which check ran (full, partial or none) and which screenshots you looked at. Don't say you looked at a screenshot you didn't open.

## Long decks (20+ slides) and long reports

- **Outline first, templates second.** Split the talk into 3–5 chapters, each with a one-sentence conclusion and a slide count, and confirm that with the user before writing. Open with an agenda (`Agenda`, `PillAgenda` or `IndexStack`), start each chapter with `SectionBreak`, and end with `Closing` repeating 2–3 takeaways.
- **Act on structure hints.** check prints `hint  deck.…` for: no agenda, few section breaks, no closing slide, the same template four or more times in a row, builds on more than a third of the slides.
- **Only what the reader needs.** For every block ask: would you open this in front of the audience? If not, cut it. Code layout, internal decisions, how it was generated and the same point said twice stay out.
- **Show, don't describe.** For "how it is used", use `ChatDemo` (a mock chat) or `AppCards` (filled-in panels); for a process, give the overview before the details. These are labelled as mock-ups on screen and in `source`; never pass them off as real records or a real product.
- **Motion with a purpose.** `MediaExpand`, `ScatterOrder`, `TiltShot`, `ConvergeShots` (several pictures gather into one) and `StepVideo` (a video that plays one segment per press) work with `build: 'reveal'`; once or twice per deck is enough.
- **Share `#/read` afterwards**: the whole deck on one scrolling page, grouped by chapter, with speaker notes.

## Common mistakes

- Picking the template first and then padding or cramming content.
- Using `lead` to restate the title.
- Putting a number on the cover that has no source and never reappears.
- Fixing overflow by shrinking fonts or adding custom styles.
- Writing text into `Closing`'s `mark`: it accepts only a 1–4 digit number; text leaves the whole slide blank.
- Changing `src/kit/catalog.ts` without running `npx pcafe docs`, leaving docs and this skill's reference tables out of date.

## If you catch yourself…

| Doing this | Do this instead |
| --- | --- |
| Shrinking type or writing CSS or a new component to make content fit | Split the slide, cut words or change the template |
| Running the full check after every small edit | Use `--changed`; run the full check once before delivery |
| Saying "done" after a partial check passed | Run the full check first |
| Saying "looks good" without opening the screenshots | Open them, then say which slides you looked at |
| Deleting an element that keeps changing to get past the check | Add `data-settle-ignore` to it |
| Reading every file in `references/en/templates/` | Read the index; open only the groups you use |
| Rerunning the same FAIL unchanged | Read where and why it failed, fix it, then rerun |
| Giving an unsourced number a source that looks real | Mark it "illustrative" or "estimate", or drop it |

## Emoji

Type emoji in any text field of any format. They render from the bundled set, not the system font. The family goes in `emojiStyle` (`fluent` by default, `flat`, `noto`); browse at `#/kit/emoji`. One or two a slide, in front of a subheading, never replacing words; keep them out of financial, legal and incident documents. Social cards can carry more, with `motif: { kind: 'emoji', e, around }` on a cover. Flags and skin tones are not bundled (build error); SVG chart labels can't hold them (QA fails). Use `flat` or `noto` for large print. See the Emoji section of the guide.

## Reports and résumés

When the user wants a Word-style report, an analysis document or a résumé, do not make slides. Read `docs/en/paper.md` first, write `src/papers/<name>/index.tsx`, run `npx pcafe paper check <name>` until PASS, then look at every `qa/<name>/page-NN.png`.

For a web article (product introduction, review, anything shared as a link), set `view: 'read'` on the report: it opens as a web page that reads well on phone and desktop, and the A4 view and PDF stay available. Give the flow with `Steps` first, then show with `Chat` and `Apps`; see `src/papers/web-guide`.

## Cards, video, courses and screens

Choose by where the result will be used. Read the doc first, and run `npx pcafe <format> check <name>` until PASS. Then look at the pictures the doc names.

| The user wants | Format | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Images to post on a social platform: a carousel, a cover, a thumbnail, a link preview | `cards` | `docs/en/cards.md` |
| An MP4 to upload, with burned-in captions and no synced voice | `video` | `docs/en/video.md` |
| A self-study lesson with exercises that explain each answer. Not an exam: the answers are in the file | `course` | `docs/en/course.md` |
| A looping page on a big screen, read from a distance with nobody presenting | `screen` | `docs/en/screen.md` |
| A piece to print or post as one long image: poster, roll-up banner, tri-fold, long infographic | `print` | `docs/en/print.md` |

A deck is still the choice when someone presents in a room. Do not embed slides in a screen: their text is too small at a distance.
