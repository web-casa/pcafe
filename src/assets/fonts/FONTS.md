All fonts in this folder are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (full text: OFL.txt).
Copyright notices are listed in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md at the repository root.

- Geist, Geist Mono, Instrument Serif, Anton, Plus Jakarta Sans, Silkscreen: from @fontsource packages.
- Space Grotesk, Newsreader, Source Serif 4, Bodoni Moda, DM Serif Display, Alfa Slab One, Archivo Narrow, Caveat Brush, Barlow, Archivo, Bowlby One, Stardos Stencil, Barlow Condensed: from @fontsource packages, for the ten styles ported from beautiful-html-templates (only the fonts of the style a deck uses are embedded in its build).
- noto-sans-sc-*, noto-serif-sc-*: subsets of Noto Sans CJK SC / Noto Serif CJK SC
  (https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk), made by scripts/subset-fonts.py.
  "Source" is a Reserved Font Name of the upstream fonts; these subsets are named "Deck Sans SC" / "Deck Serif SC".
